# Email templates (English): short research-fit outreach

Rules of thumb:
- Keep it under 180 words.
- Put one concrete research hook in the first two lines (a paper, lab note, or opening they posted). Never the same first sentence twice.
- Attach your CV (PDF). Add the sworn English transcript when you have it.
- Be upfront about the Tecnólogo. Hiding it just gets you rejected later at the graduate-studies office.
- Send each email one by one, to a professor whose recent papers actually match your work. Mail-merge blasts are easy to spot.
- Send Tue–Thu morning in the recipient's time zone. Follow up once after 10–14 days, then stop.
- Voice: write like Alexandre, not a bot. No travessões. No identical paragraphs across recipients. Do not paste spam-filter keywords from one lab into another subject (except when that lab's own page asks for a specific word, e.g. Bezemer "hasperat").
- Contact block: plain name + email + LinkedIn on separate lines. No pipe-separated link rows. Do not use a joking Gmail display name for academic mail.

Duration line (eligibility / office emails):
"My undergraduate credential is a Brazilian Tecnólogo in Computing from FMU in São Paulo (about 2.5 years of study from 18 to 23, with the usual mid-December to mid-January and June breaks). IQAS and WES generally rate it as a 2 to 3 year post-secondary diploma."

Shorter flag (professor emails):
"my undergraduate credential is a Brazilian Tecnólogo in Computing (FMU), usually rated as a 3-year diploma rather than a 4-year bachelor's"

### Notes per school
- **SFU** asks applicants not to email about waiving requirements. Skip Variant B there and use Variant A only with a matched professor. Make the experience case in the SOP.
- **U of Alberta** says it doesn't pre-screen eligibility. Use Variant A with professors only, before **Dec 15, 2026**.
- **Athabasca** (fstgrad@athabascau.ca): Variant B fits. The equivalency argument has to go in writing with the application anyway.
- **U of Regina** wants applicants to email potential supervisors before applying. They require a WES report.
