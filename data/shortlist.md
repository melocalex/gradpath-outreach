# Shortlist: Canadian master's programs (AI / CS / IT / SE) for a Brazilian Tecnólogo holder

Researched 5 Nov 2026 (BRT, UTC-3). **Plausibility** says how likely a Tecnólogo (assessed as a 2- or 3-year diploma) clears the *minimum education* bar:
- **A**: there is an explicit written route (equivalency, exception or professional experience)
- **B**: a qualifying year, a bridge, or "3-year may apply"
- **C**: needs a 4-year bachelor's. Listed because of Calgary/Alberta location or strong AI; it's a reach or a target for after you finish a degree.

How to read the table:
- "Not confirmed" means I didn't find it on an official page, so don't use it until you've checked.
- Deadlines are for **international** applicants unless noted.
- Tuition and funding: TA/RA = teaching/research assistantship.
